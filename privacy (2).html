Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what Donut Utils ("the bot") collects and how it's used. We keep it to what the bot needs to work.

What we collect

  • Discord IDs: user IDs, server IDs and channel IDs, used to run commands and apply settings.
  • Command input: what you type into a command, such as a Minecraft username or item name, used to fetch your result.
  • Technical logs: timestamps, command names and error messages, used to fix bugs and keep the bot running.
  • Saved settings: anything a server or user chooses to save with the bot.

What we don't collect

We don't collect passwords, payment details or private messages. The bot only reads messages that are commands for it.

How we use it

Data is used only to run the bot's features, fix problems and prevent abuse. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Third parties

The bot runs on Discord, and some lookups use outside data sources, so the text you submit with a command may be sent to them to get a result. Their own privacy policies apply, including Discord's.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a private server and kept only as long as needed. Logs are cleared regularly. If the bot is removed from a server, that server's saved data may be deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete the data tied to your Discord ID or your server by contacting us in the support server. You can also remove the bot from your server at any time.

Children

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement to use the bot. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions? Ask in the support server.
